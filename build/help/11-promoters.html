Promoters and coordinates

An E-box is only a circadian switch if it sits in the right place — the promoter. Here you learn how genomic positions are written down, and how to pull a promoter's DNA out of the reference genome.

The promoter window

For every gene we defined the promoter as the window from 1000 letters before the TSS to 500 after — 1500 letters in all, measured along the direction the gene is read. Those windows are saved as a BED file, the standard way to write "a stretch of a chromosome":

head /data/examples/genes/promoters.bed
Reading a BED line

chr2 166127019 166128519 SCN1A 0 - means chromosome 2, from position 166,127,019 to 166,128,519, for gene SCN1A, on the - strand. BED counts from 0 and the end is "one past the last base" (so this is 1500 bases). Many bioinformatics mistakes are off-by-one errors from mixing this up with 1-based counting — watch for it.

Pull out a promoter's sequence

We also saved the DNA of every promoter as a FASTA file, promoters.fa, one record per gene. samtools faidx fetches one by name:

samtools faidx /data/examples/reference/promoters.fa SCN1A

The header line records where the sequence came from — the chromosome, the genomic start and end, and the strand. You will need that genomic start later to turn a position within the promoter into a position on the chromosome.

How GC-rich are these promoters?

Promoters near a TSS are often rich in C and G. That matters for E-boxes, because CACGTG is itself C/G-heavy. Measure it with seqtk:

seqtk comp /data/examples/reference/promoters.fa | awk '{gc+=$4+$5; tot+=$2} END{printf "overall GC = %.1f%%\n", 100*gc/tot}'

Keep that number in mind: when you ask whether E-boxes are "more common than chance", chance has to mean chance given this GC content, not a flat 25% per letter.

Checkpoint

There are 641 promoters (the 21 channelopathy and 632 epilepsy genes, de-duplicated, with one mitochondrial gene dropped), each 1500 bases long, and the overall GC content is about 59%. If your numbers match, your reference and your coordinates line up and you are ready to hunt for E-boxes.
