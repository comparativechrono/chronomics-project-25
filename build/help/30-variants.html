Human variation in the promoters

Now bring in real people. The 1000 Genomes Project sequenced the genomes of thousands of individuals from around the world. You have every variant that falls in your promoters — and you can ask how common each one is, and where.

The data

We pulled every single-letter variant (SNV) in the 641 promoters from the 1000 Genomes 30× data — 3,202 genomes from 26 populations () — into one small file:

zcat /data/examples/variants/promoter_variants.vcf.gz | grep -v "^##" | head -5

This is a VCF file, the standard format for variants. Each line is one variant: its chromosome and position, the reference letter (REF), the alternative letter (ALT), and an INFO field. We put the useful numbers in INFO: GENE, the overall allele frequency AF, and the frequency in each of the five super-populations (AF_AFR, AF_AMR, AF_EAS, AF_EUR, AF_SAS).

Allele frequency, and why populations matter

Allele frequency is the fraction of chromosomes in a group that carry the alternative letter. AF=0.5 means half; AF=0.0003 means it was seen on just a couple of chromosomes. A variant can be common in one population and absent in another — which is why a single global number can mislead, and why the file keeps each super-population separately.

Count and summarise with bcftools

bcftools is the standard tool for VCFs. How many variants are there, and how many are rare?

bcftools view -H /data/examples/variants/promoter_variants.vcf.gz | wc -l
# how many are rare (overall AF < 0.5%)?
bcftools query -f '%INFO/AF\n' /data/examples/variants/promoter_variants.vcf.gz \
  | awk '$1<0.005{r++} END{print r" rare of "NR}'
Checkpoint

There are about 22,836 SNVs across the 641 promoters, and the large majority are rare. That skew — far more rare than common variants — is normal for functional regions, and it is a clue you will use in Part 4 when you test for selection.

Pull a variant into a notebook

Load the variants into Python as a table so you can join them to your E-boxes next:

import pandas as pd, subprocess
cols = ["chrom","pos","vid","ref","alt","gene","AF","AF_AFR","AF_AMR","AF_EAS","AF_EUR","AF_SAS"]
q = "%CHROM\t%POS\t%ID\t%REF\t%ALT\t%INFO/GENE\t%INFO/AF\t%INFO/AF_AFR\t%INFO/AF_AMR\t%INFO/AF_EAS\t%INFO/AF_EUR\t%INFO/AF_SAS\n"
out = subprocess.run(["bcftools","query","-f",q,
      "/data/examples/variants/promoter_variants.vcf.gz"], capture_output=True, text=True).stdout
var = pd.read_csv(pd.io.common.StringIO(out), sep="\t", names=cols, na_values=".")
print(var.shape); var.head()
