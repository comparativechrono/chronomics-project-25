Write it up — so someone could repeat it

A result that cannot be repeated is not yet a result. Your write-up should let another person (or you, in six months) reproduce every number. Here is how to structure it, and what to hand in.

Keep a running record as you go

Don't leave this to the end. In your notebook, after each step, write a short Markdown cell: what you did, which file and which command, and the number you got. Those cells are your methods section. The habit — recording how each result was produced — is the single most valued skill in computational biology (and the focus of our group's work on reproducibility).

Structure of the report

IntroductionThe clock, E-boxes, and why epilepsy/channelopathy genes. One paragraph, citing the reading list.
MethodsGene panels (PanelApp, versions), promoter definition (TSS −1000/+500), E-box definition (exact CACGTG and PWM ≥ 0.85, say which matrix), variant source (1000 Genomes 30×), conservation (phyloP 470-way), and your control (CpG-matched). State every threshold.
ResultsEnrichment (observed vs expected), the catalogue size, the selection test (figure + statistic), and the shortlist. Report numbers with what they are compared to.
DiscussionWhat it means, the confounds you noted, and what the 100KGP follow-up would test.

State your limitations plainly

  • A PWM match and conservation predict a functional switch; they do not prove CLOCK:BMAL1 binds it in neurons.
  • The conservation signal is partly confounded by promoter position near the TSS (you controlled for CpGs, not for everything).
  • You used the 1000 Genomes reference populations, not patients; frequencies are population frequencies, not disease associations.
  • Your results depend on thresholds (PWM score, promoter window). Showing they hold at a second threshold is worth a sentence.

What to hand in

  • Your notebook(s), downloaded as .ipynb (Notebook tab → Download), with the Markdown records.
  • Your output files — eboxes_canonical.bed, catalogue.csv, positions.csv, your figure — via My files (top right) → download all as a .zip.
  • The written report.
