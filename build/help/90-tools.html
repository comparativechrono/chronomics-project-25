The tools, and getting help

A short reference to the programs used in this project. They are the standard tools, compiled to run in your browser (), so the commands you type run on the actual data files, on your own computer.

samtools faidxPulls a named sequence (here, a promoter) out of a FASTA file.
bcftoolsReads, filters and queries VCF variant files (view, query).
bedtools intersectFinds overlaps between two sets of genomic regions (here, variants and E-boxes).
seqtkQuick sequence statistics, such as GC content (seqtk comp).
PythonIn the Notebook tab: pandas for tables, matplotlib for plots and scipy for statistics. Most of the motif scanning and the catalogue are done in Python.
RAlso in the Notebook tab: ggplot2 for publication-quality figures, and R's built-in statistical tests.
DatabasesLive Ensembl and NCBI searches, for checking a gene or a variant.
Genome browserIGV, for viewing your variants, E-boxes and conservation along the genome (see The genome browser).

Getting help with a program

In the terminal, help lists every program, and most programs accept --help. (bedtools prints its help as if it were an error message, so 2>&1 is needed to pass it through head with everything else.)

help
bcftools --help
bedtools intersect --help 2>&1 | head -40

Record the versions you used, because they belong in your methods:

samtools --version | head -1
bcftools --version | head -1
bedtools --version
