The tools, and getting help

A short reference to the programs this project uses. They are the real tools, compiled to run in your browser (); your commands really run on real files, on this computer.

samtools faidxPull a named sequence (a promoter) out of a FASTA file.
bcftoolsRead, filter and query VCF variant files (view, query).
bedtools intersectFind overlaps between two sets of genomic regions (variants × E-boxes).
seqtkQuick sequence statistics, such as GC content (seqtk comp).
PythonIn the Notebook tab: pandas for tables, matplotlib for plots, scipy for statistics. Your motif scanning and the catalogue live here.
RAlso in the Notebook tab: ggplot2 for publication-style figures and built-in statistical tests.
DatabasesLive Ensembl / NCBI lookups to check a gene or variant.
Genome browserIGV: your variants, E-boxes and conservation along the genome (The genome browser).

Getting help on a program

In the terminal, help lists every program, and most take --help (bedtools prints its help as if it were an error, so 2>&1 sends it through head with the rest):

help
bcftools --help
bedtools intersect --help 2>&1 | head -40

Record the version you used — it belongs in your methods:

samtools --version | head -1
bcftools --version | head -1
bedtools --version
