The genome browser

The Genome browser tab runs IGV () with the human reference genome GRCh38 (hg38), the same build as all the project data. The genome is loaded from the IGV team's servers, so this tab needs an internet connection.

What you see

Three project tracks are shown above the genome's own gene track (Refseq All, at the bottom):

Project promoters (TSS −1000/+500)The 641 promoter windows, from /data/examples/genes/promoters.bed. Click a box to see its gene.
1000 Genomes promoter SNVsEvery promoter variant, from /data/examples/variants/promoter_variants.vcf.gz. Click a variant to see its allele frequencies (AF, AF_EUR and so on).
phyloP 470-way (mammals)Conservation at every base. Bars above the line are positions that have changed little across mammals; bars below it are positions that have changed quickly. The scores are read a section at a time from UCSC's copy of the genome-wide file, and are the same as those in conservation/phylop_promoters.csv.gz.

Moving around

Type a gene (SCN1A) or a position (chr2:166,127,520-166,129,020) into the box at the top of the browser and press Enter. Drag sideways to move along the genome, and use + and − to zoom. When you zoom in far enough, the individual bases appear. For example:

Add your own tracks

Files you have made, such as your E-boxes or your catalogue saved as a BED file, can be shown as tracks too. In the Genome browser tab, Add my files… lists the BED, VCF and bedGraph files in your home folder. Once you have made eboxes_canonical.bed in Find the E-boxes, you can also use the button below. Chromosome names must be written the same way as here (chr2, not 2), and positions must be on GRCh38.

Saving a picture

Save image downloads the current view as a PNG file, which is useful for your report (say which region it shows).

If it doesn't load

The genome and the conservation track are loaded over the internet. If the tab says the genome browser could not start, check your connection and press Try again, or reload the page. The rest of the workbench works without it.
