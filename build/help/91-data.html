The project data

Everything you need is in /data/examples, read-only. It was prepared from public sources (listed below) so you never have to download anything large. Here is each file.

genes/brain_channelopathy.tsv21 green genes of the PanelApp "Brain channelopathy" panel, with Ensembl ID, chromosome, strand, TSS and promoter window.
genes/epilepsy_all.tsv632 green genes of "Early-onset or syndromic epilepsy" (includes the 21).
genes/promoters.bedThe 641 promoter windows (TSS −1000/+500), as a BED file on GRCh38.
reference/promoters.fa (+ .fai)The DNA of every promoter (forward strand). Each header records the gene, its chromosome:start-end and strand.
variants/promoter_variants.vcf.gz (+ .tbi)Every single-letter variant in the promoters, from 1000 Genomes 30×, with overall and per-super-population allele frequencies in INFO.
conservation/phylop_promoters.csv.gzphyloP conservation (470-way mammalian and 100-way vertebrate) for every promoter base.
motif/ARNTL_MA0603.2.jasparThe BMAL1 (Arntl) binding matrix from JASPAR, for the PWM scan.

GRCh38 throughout

Every coordinate here is on the GRCh38 human reference. If you ever bring in outside data, check it is GRCh38 too — mixing genome builds is a classic and silent source of wrong answers.

Where it came from

  • Gene panels — Genomics England PanelApp ().
  • Gene models, promoter coordinates and sequence — Ensembl / GENCODE ().
  • Variants — the 1000 Genomes Project 30× release, 3,202 genomes ().
  • Conservation — phyloP, Zoonomia 470-way ().
  • Motif — JASPAR ().
