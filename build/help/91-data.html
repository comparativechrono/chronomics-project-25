The project data

Everything you need is in /data/examples, which is read-only. It was prepared from the public sources listed below, so you never need to download anything large. These are the files:

genes/brain_channelopathy.tsvThe 21 green genes of the PanelApp "Brain channelopathy" panel, with Ensembl ID, chromosome, strand, TSS and promoter window.
genes/epilepsy_all.tsvAll 641 genes in the project: the 632 green genes of the "Early-onset or syndromic epilepsy" panel plus the 21 channelopathy genes, each listed once, without one mitochondrial gene. The panel column says which list each gene comes from.
genes/promoters.bedThe 641 promoter windows (TSS −1000/+500, 1501 bases each), as a BED file on GRCh38.
reference/promoters.fa (+ .fai)The DNA sequence of every promoter (forward strand). Each header gives the gene, its chromosome:start-end, its strand and its TSS.
variants/promoter_variants.vcf.gz (+ .tbi)Every single-base variant in the promoters, from the 1000 Genomes 30× data, with overall and super-population allele frequencies in INFO.
conservation/phylop_promoters.csv.gzphyloP conservation scores (470-way mammalian and 100-way vertebrate) for every promoter base.
motif/ARNTL_MA0603.2.jasparThe BMAL1 (Arntl) binding matrix from JASPAR, used for the PWM scan.

GRCh38 throughout

All the coordinates here are on the GRCh38 human reference genome. If you bring in data from elsewhere, check that it is on GRCh38 too. Mixing genome builds is a common cause of wrong answers, and nothing will warn you when it happens.
