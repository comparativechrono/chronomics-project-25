Reading list

Start with one or two papers from "The clock and E-boxes" and from our group's work, then read the others as you reach the parts of the project that need them. Each paper that is available online links to its DOI.

The clock and E-boxes

How CLOCK:BMAL1 controls the timing of genes through E-boxes, from reviews to genome-wide binding maps.

Chronomic medicine, chronotype and seizures

Why the clock matters for disease, the genetics of being a morning or evening person, and the links between epilepsy, sleep and the clock.

From our group

The work on E-boxes in SCN5A and cardiac arrhythmia that this project grew out of, and related papers on chronomic medicine.

Data and methods

The 1000 Genomes Project, the gene panels, the conservation scores and the motif database. Cite the ones you use in your methods.

The software

The programs you run, and the projects that make them run in a browser. Cite them with the versions you used (The tools shows how to find them).
